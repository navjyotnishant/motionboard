## What the pack licence means

- Noncommercial use is free: personal projects, study, research, hobby videos. Changing the packs for that use is fine.
- Commercial use (selling the packs, shipping them in a paid product or service, or work done for money) needs a separate licence from the author.
- Keep the `NOTICE` file with any copy.
- Whether a monetised channel counts as commercial use is decided by the PolyForm terms, not by this page. Read [modes/LICENSE](../modes/LICENSE) or ask the author.

## Model licences

`mb setup` lists each model with its licence link. LTX-2.3: https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.3/blob/main/LICENSE (free under USD 10M annual revenue). LTX-2.5 and its detail add-on: https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/LICENSE-2_x. The detail add-on is gated, so you accept its licence on Hugging Face before downloading. Motionboard never bundles weights, and the installer downloads them only after showing the size and licence and getting a yes.
